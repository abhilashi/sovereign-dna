## 2026-09-01 - [A11y] Added ARIA labels to zoom controls\n**Learning:** Icon-only zoom controls in visualizations (like GenomeMap) are inaccessible to screen readers and lack hover context for sighted users without `aria-label` and `title`.\n**Action:** Always include `aria-label` and `title` on icon-only buttons like + and - zoom controls to ensure both accessibility and general usability.
